    HOW THE YEAST MODEL WORKS

    Transparent, not magic.

    Pizza Lab follows the dough's temperature hour by hour. It starts at your final dough temperature and drifts toward the room, fridge or proof temperature at a realistic speed (a bulk mass is slower than single balls). Yeast activity at each temperature is added up into equivalent hours at 25 °C, and the yeast dose is solved from that.

    activity(T) = ((T + 1) ÷ 26)² · IDY % = 0.93 × salt factor × your calibration ÷ (equiv. hours − 1.25)^1.3

    The constants were fitted to the published recipes below. Bread-style recipes that aim for a fast, generous rise use more yeast than the model — that's expected.
