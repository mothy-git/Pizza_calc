CALCULATION BASIS

What Pizza Lab is actually calculating.

Ingredient weights: standard baker's percentages. Flour is 100%; every other ingredient is a percentage of flour. Pizza Lab solves backwards from your target total dough weight and includes yeast in the total, so the finished formula still adds up to the ball weights you requested.

Flour = target dough ÷ (1 + water% + salt% + yeast% + oil% + sugar%)

Preferments: the selected percentage of total flour and matching water are moved into the poolish/biga. The final dough water is reduced by the same amount so overall hydration does not change.

Yeast: the automatic yeast estimate is a Pizza Lab model, not an official AVPN/Ooni formula. It is anchored to two current Ooni recipes — a short 3–4 hour dough and a ~25 hour Neapolitan-style dough — then temperature-weighted. Cold fermentation is deliberately suppressed below 10°C. Treat it as a starting dose and calibrate it to your fridge, flour and dough temperature.

WHAT IS SOURCE-BACKED VS GUIDANCE?
SOURCEDirectly derived from an official association, manufacturer or Ooni published recipe.
GUIDANCEPizza Lab working range or practical recommendation. Useful, but not an official standard.
MODELCalculated estimate, especially yeast/temperature behaviour. Tune against your own bake journal.
